# StripCards

A row of equal cards separated by 1px hairlines (a 1px gap over a `rule` background), each with a mono kicker, an uppercase title and short copy.

**The consumer provides** two to four `.home-strip__card` blocks: a `p.kicker` (`/section`), an `h3.home-strip__title` (`card-title` scale) and a `.home-strip__body` of one or two paragraphs. Mark at most one card `--accent`: it fills with `violet`, all text turns `bg`, and its last paragraph goes bold.

**Rules** the same hairline-grid technique builds the board grid, Get Involved tiles, donate cards and in-kind sponsor grid; reuse it rather than adding gaps or rounded cards.
