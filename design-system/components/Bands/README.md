# Bands

Full-bleed colour sections that break the dark page: coral and violet CTA bands, and the light band.

**Variants**
- `.cta-band--coral`: the employer-match and donate bands. Ink `bg` (6.6:1).
- `.cta-band--violet`: the Get Involved band and the accent strip card. Ink `bg` (6.9:1).
- `.light-band`: `band-bg` ground with `band-text` ink; links switch to `violet-ink` (5.9:1). Used for sponsors, the grant Application and "How your donation helps".

**The consumer provides** a heading (`section-h2` scale) and a sentence or two. Padding is `section-pad` × `gutter`.

**Rules** never place two bands of the same colour next to each other, never put coral text or a coral button on violet (1.0:1), and inside a light band use `band-rule` for hairlines and a 2px `band-text` top rule above sub-lists.
